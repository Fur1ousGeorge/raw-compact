Raw Compact Support

Raw Compact is a Mac app that makes RAW video from the Nikon ZR smaller while every file stays RAW.

Privacy Policy
Contact

Email rawcompact@gmail.com. Please include your Mac model, macOS version and the app version (Settings → About). We usually reply within two business days.

Which files work?

R3D NE (.R3D) and N-RAW (.NEV) clips recorded on the Nikon ZR.

What does the free trial do?

It exports whole clips with a visible watermark, so you can judge the results in your own timeline. One purchase removes the watermark.

How do I restore my purchase on another Mac?

Open Settings → Your copy → Restore purchase while signed in to the same Apple Account.

A clip won't open or compress

Choose "Make support excerpt" from the clip's ••• menu and email us the small file it creates. Your original is never modified.

Requirements

A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 14 or later.
