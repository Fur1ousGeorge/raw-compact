Raw Compact Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026 · Support

Raw Compact does not collect, store or share any personal data. The app has no account and no analytics, and it does not send your footage or information about it anywhere. Everything happens on your Mac.

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. Apple's privacy policy covers that process.

The app keeps its settings, a list of finished clips and clip thumbnails on your Mac, in its own storage. Deleting the app removes them.

If you email us for support, we use your message only to answer it.

Questions: rawcompact@gmail.com
